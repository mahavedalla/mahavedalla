# Whether sensual stimulation is the fetter?

## Bibliography

<!-- 

Notes:

I'm inclined to say no. Again, sensual stimulation here does not refer to the sense object itself, nor the pleasure of gratifying a craving through the sense object, but the pleasure of the object, a pleasure which remains in the total absence of craving. As the last comment suggests, the sensual stimulation, or just sensual pleasure is not the fetter itself. It is contingently a fetter insofar as indulging sensual stimulation exacerbates the fetter, namely, craving, among the other defilements.
