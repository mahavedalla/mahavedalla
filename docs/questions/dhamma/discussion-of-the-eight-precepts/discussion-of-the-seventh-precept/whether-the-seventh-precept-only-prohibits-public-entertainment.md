Objection 1: It seems the seventh precept only prohibits public entertainment, for in the origin story to the establishment of the corresponding precept in the monk's discipline, the group of bhikkhus attended a public festival, wherein there was music and such like. Thus, understood in light of its origin, the seventh precept seems to only prohibit similar such behaviors as the aforesaid: namely, public indulgence in entertainment?
