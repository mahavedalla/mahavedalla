You Are Not Redundant: "Wealth doesn’t have inherent value, nor does status, nor does praise, nor do sensual pleasures."

Strictly speaking, as I currently understand the terms "inherent" and "intrinsic", they are not identical in meaning, though they are often interchanged. It would be unreasonable to assume that when Taan Ajaan uses such a term that he is using it with its specifically philosophical connotations and implications. Thus what he means precisely should probably be fleshed out. Nonetheless, this is a very close answer.
