The Mahāvedalla (MV) is a repository which attempts to systematically and philosophically ask and answer as many questions as possible relevant to Buddhadhamma and Vinaya.
At this time, it is a personal repository, written for my benefit alone in my attempt to systematically and philosophically understand Buddhadhamma. It is not currently written with the intent of publication for any audience, though I understand publication is not excluded as a future possibility. Nonetheless, it is intended to be written in such a style as if it were going to be published.
This comment is made to absolve me of any responsibility for any confusion, misrepresentation, and such like, which could arise in one reading this, owing to a failure of mine (such as in knowledge of source texts, inference, etc.), which I am most anxious to avoid. MV is simply my attempt to flesh out, as it were, Buddhadhamma following the aforesaid aim.

Various tools will be employed to avoid falsity and misrepresenation.

Extensive usage of various linguistic devices will be employed: evidentials, credentials, scope indicators, and endorsement indicators. I feel it is essential to studying the MV that one be well accustomed to recognizing these devices and their import. They are employed to avoid, as far as is possible, false statements and missrepresentations which might otherwise arise.

Evidentials: Indicators of the source of or type of justification for some proposition.

1. Sensory; as in "I saw that he arrived"
2. Inferential; as in "I induce that excess consumption of sugar is unhealthy"
Reportative; "I heard from so and so that he is competent for the job" 
Convictive; "I believe that Jesus rose from the dead"
Intuitive; "I intuit that innocent murder is wrong"
Recollective; "I recall that he said he would be here soon"

Credentials: Indicators of credence.

0-100%

"confidently," "not confidently," "probably," etc., as in "I feel confidently that this is true."

Scope indicators: Indicators of the extent of the application of some proposition.

"to the extent that," "insofar as," "as long as," etc., as in "He can be called an ascetic, TO THE EXTENT THAT he practices for the subdual of all but necessary desires."

Endorsement indicators: Indicators of the speaker's endorsement of some proposition.

"sympathetic to," "proponent of," etc., as in "I am sympathetic to the an inflationary view." The difference between this and credence is not entirely clear.
