Why renounce sensuality?

For the attenuation and abandonment of sensual craving?

Why attenuate and abandon sensual craving?

For the attenuation and abandonment of stress and suffering.

How does attenuating and abandoning sensual craving attenuate and abandon stress and suffering?

Sensual craving is a species of craving, which is (one of) the principal cause(s) of stress and suffering.

How is sensual craving the cause of stress and suffering?

"How" in epistemic sense: That sensual craving is the cause of stress and suffering can be known in various ways: (1) reflection on one's own experience of craving, particularly when frustrated; (2) reflection on that of others; (3) counterfactual questions such as, "If my desire(s) were to be frustrated, would I suffer"; (4) Mindfulness of craving itself, whether frustrated or not; (5) abstaining from indulging craving.

"How" in ontological sense: It is part of the essence of craving that it is rooted in stress and dissatisfaction. This is primitive.

Yet, this requires renouncing sensual pleasures which bring one pleasure?

Concede that some raw sensual pleasures are in fact pleasures in themselves. However, also, various sensual experiences regarded as pleasant are in fact unpleasant, but are felt as pleasant owning to sense faculties distorted by craving.

Nonetheless, beyond this concession, the predominant pleasure resulting from and reason for indulging in sensual pleasures is the remediation of the stress inherent in craving; wherefore, said pleasure is not a positive good in its own right and one is better off free from that very craving in the first place; and since the indulgence of that sensual pleasure exacerbates said craving, one is made worse off thereby.

Yet, it does not seem like sensual craving causes much stress, since when people's cravings are frustrated, they seem to move on an get over it?

The Suppressant Effect of the Indulgence of Sensual Craving: Presumably, the subject referred to in the question is someone who by and large satisfies their cravings, but occasionally fails to. Since their cravings are by and large satisfied, the occasional frustration thereof will result in a suppressed effect, as when someone misses a dose of a medication, the symptoms they experience might be suppressed or absent altogether. In other words, the stress experienced by the occasional frustration of sensual craving in an average person is not a representative sample of the stress underlying sensual craving. To see that, undertake systematic renunciation.
