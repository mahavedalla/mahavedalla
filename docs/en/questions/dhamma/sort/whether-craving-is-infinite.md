# Whether craving is infinite?

## Bibliography

<!-- 

Notes:

Two infinities might be distinguished: actual and potential. The former is taken to refer to an actually infinite quantity of concrete things or some concrete thing. The latter to a quantity which is finite, but increasing towards infinity as a limit. Two ways in which craving might be infinite are distinguished: in intensity and quantity. It is not clear what Canonical references there are for this topic. If saṃsāra is regarded as beginningless, than perhaps an actually infinite quantity of craving is entailed. It seems for independent reasons saṃsāra might not be regarded as temporally infinite in the future (though perhaps neither as finite; subject for another article). Nonetheless, if it were, this might entail at least a potential infinity was the wheel of saṃsāra moves on ad infinitum. What might also be incorporated is whether craving is being relativized to individuals or not: i.e., can an individual in some sense be subjected to infinite craving, or can craving merely be infinite across groups of individuals.  It seems the former claim is stroner.
