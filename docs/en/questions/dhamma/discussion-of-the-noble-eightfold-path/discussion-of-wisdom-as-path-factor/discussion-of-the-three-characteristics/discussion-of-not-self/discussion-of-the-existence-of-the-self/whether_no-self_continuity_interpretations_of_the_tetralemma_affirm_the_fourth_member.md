# Whether no-self continuity interpretations of the tetralemma affirm the fourth member?

## Bibliography

Notes:

tatth'āyaṃ catukoṭi: "evaṃ vimuttacitto pana, bho gotama, bhikkhu kuhiṃ upapajjatī"ti? "upapajjatīti kho, vaccha, na upeti". "tena hi, bho gotama, na upapajjatī"ti? "na upapajjatīti kho, vaccha, na upeti". "tena hi, bho gotama, upapajjati ca na ca upapajjatī"ti? "upapajjati ca na ca upapajjatīti kho, vaccha, na upeti". "tena hi, bho gotama, neva upapajjati na na upapajjatī"ti? "neva upapajjati na na upapajjatīti kho, vaccha, na upetī"ti

keci "evaṃ vimuttacitto pana, bho gotama, bhikkhu kuhiṃ 'upapajjatī'tiādi: idha paramatthaṃ pubbe maraṇā vijjantaṃ tathāgatassa attaṃ maññitvā pucchati. yassa yena upapajjanacoditā, so eva paramatthato nupalabbhati yathā vañjhātanayassa matteyyatākittanan"ti.

keci "taṃ na sundaraṃ. kasmā? catutthakoṭiyā upetatāpajjanato. tathāgato ce nupalabbhati, kiṃ so upapajjissati, tasmā paṭhamāya ca dutiyāya ca koṭiyā micchattā. atha "neva upapajjati na na upapajjatī"ti āpajjanato, catutthakoṭi upeti. na pana upeti, yathāha:

'neva upapajjati na na upapajjatīti kho, vaccha, na upetī'ti."

nevāhaṃ idaṃ abhinandāmi na paṭikkosāmi. atthi pana me eko niggaho paccavekkhitabbo.

tattha siyā "tathāgatassa anupalabbhanato paṭhamāya dutiyāya ca anupetatā, na micchatā. tena hi catutthāya anupetatā, na saccatā. evaṃ catukoṭiyā ekamekā rakkhitā"ti.
