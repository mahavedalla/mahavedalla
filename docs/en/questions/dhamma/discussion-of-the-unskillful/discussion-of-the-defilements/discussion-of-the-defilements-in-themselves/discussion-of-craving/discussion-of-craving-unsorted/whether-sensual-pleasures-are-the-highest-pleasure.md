# Whether sensual pleasures are the highest pleasure?

## Bibliography

<!-- 

Notes:

It is worth discussing here the views of other religions and philosophies. It seems that all but the fringe elements of the world's major religions agree that sensual pleasures are not the highest pleasures. It must be stated this is not to contend these religions agree with any other elements of the view of sensual pleasure found in the Canon; this is undetermined by this question. Moreover, this is not to say these religions maintain that sensual pleasures are in any problematic; on the contrary, it might be that some major world religions to some extent include sensual pleasures in the domain of the sacred. Such are topics for other questions. 

As for philosophies, this is less clear. My faint understanding of Aristotelian, Platonic, and Stoic thought gives me the hint that the conclusion is similar, but this hasn't been verified.
