# Whether sensual craving has drawbacks?

## Introduction

There are at least five distinct, though related, families of drawbacks of sensuality:

1. Mental drawbacks
    1. The suffering of frustrated sensual craving
    2. The suffering inherent in sensual craving
    3. The unsatisfactoriness of sensual craving
2. Obstructive drawbacks
    1. Hinders the development of the superior pleasures concentration
    2. Weakens wisdom
3. Ethical drawbacks
    1. Leads to unethical conduct
    2. Hinders universal love 
4. Aesthetic drawbacks
5. Practical drawbacks
    1. Sensual craving consumes time
    2. And consumes resources

Let us examine each family and its constituents in turn.
