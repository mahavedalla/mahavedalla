# Whether an Arahant still strives even after attaining Arahantship?

## Bibliography

<!-- 

Notes:

MN 70: 

"ye te, bhikkhave, bhikkhū arahanto khīṇāsavā vusitavanto katakaraṇīyā ohitabhārā anuppattasadatthā parikkhīṇabhavasaṃyojanā sammadaññā vimuttā, tathārūpānāhaṃ, bhikkhave, bhikkhūnaṃ ‘na appamādena karaṇīya’nti vadāmi."

"Monks, those monks who have destroyed the taints, fulfilled (the holy life), done what had to be done, have laid down the burden, attained their own welfare, destroyed the fetter of being, and are liberated through right knowledge, of such monks I say 'It doesn't need to be striven for with heedfulness.'"
