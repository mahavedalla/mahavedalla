# Whether no-self continuity interpretations of the tetralemma affirm the fourth member?

tatth'āyaṃ catukoṭi: "evaṃ vimuttacitto pana, bho gotama, bhikkhu kuhiṃ upapajjatī"ti? "upapajjatīti kho, vaccha, na upeti". "tena hi, bho gotama, na upapajjatī"ti? "na upapajjatīti kho, vaccha, na upeti". "tena hi, bho gotama, upapajjati ca na ca upapajjatī"ti? "upapajjati ca na ca upapajjatīti kho, vaccha, na upeti". "tena hi, bho gotama, neva upapajjati na na upapajjatī"ti? "neva upapajjati na na upapajjatīti kho, vaccha, na upetī"ti

keci "evaṃ vimuttacitto pana, bho gotama, bhikkhu kuhiṃ 'upapajjatī'tiādi: so pubbe maraṇā vijjantaṃ tathāgatassa attaṃ maññitvā pucchati. yassa yena upapajjanacoditā, so eva paramatthato nupalabbhati yathā vañjhātanayassa matteyyatākittanaṃ. tena hi, "upapajjatī"tiādi na upetī"ti.

keci "taṃ na sundaraṃ. kasmā? catutthakoṭiyā upetatāpajjanato. tathāgato ce nupalabbhati, kiṃ so upapajjissati vā na vā upapajjissati. tasmā paṭhamadutiyāya koṭiyā micchattā. atha "neva upapajjati na na upapajjatī"ti āpajjanato, catutthakoṭi upeti. na pana upeti, yathāha:

'neva upapajjati na na upapajjatīti kho, vaccha, na upetī'ti."

nevāhaṃ idaṃ abhinandāmi na paṭikkosāmi. ayaṃ pana me vicāraṇā.

tattha siyā "tathāgatassa anupalabbhanato paṭhamadutiyāya anupetatā, na micchatā. tena hi catutthāya anupetatā, na saccatā. evaṃ catukoṭi rakkhitā"ti.
