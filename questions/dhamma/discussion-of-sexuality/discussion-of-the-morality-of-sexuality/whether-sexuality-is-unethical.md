Notes:

The scope of this question is categorical, lay or monastic or other, and refers to sexuality as such. A related question is whether sexuality is not to be pursued, in the same scope and sense.

Regarding the asevitabba question. What would need to be shown to definitively answer the question?

For instance, the following is a sketch of an argument one could give (not endorsed or rejected):

all sexual activity leads to the increase of unskillful qualities and decline of skillful ones, and is rooted in mental defilements

If something leads to..., then it is asevitabba.

therefore, all sexual activity is asevitabba.

At first glance, it seems the argument needs to be modified to account for unconsensual sexual activity.
