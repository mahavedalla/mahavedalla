# Whether there is a tendency of the Dhamma to laicization?

## Bibliography

<!-- 

Notes:

This is not intended to refer to the fact of monastics laicizing and becoming lay people, but rather the Dhamma and Vinaya being repurposed for the sake of the mundane needs and interests of lay people, as opposed to being used for its liberative function. For example, the Dhamma and Vinaya being used to help lay people navigate their relationships with in-laws.
