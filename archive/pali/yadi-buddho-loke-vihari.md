# Yadi Buddho loke vihari?

Tassa pañhassa vissajjanaṃ aṅguttaranikāye nāgasutte "tatheva loke sujāto, buddho loke viharati, nupalippati lokena, toyena padumaṃ yathā"tiādi evaṃ vuttanayena veditabbaṃ. Tasmā, "Amhākaṃ Buddho loke vihari, na so pana lokena upalippatī"ti kathetuṃ yujjati.
