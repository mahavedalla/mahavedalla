# Whether love is a defilement?

## Bibliography

<!-- 

Notes:

It currently seems to me that "pema" like "chandas" is a multi-faceted term, which cannot be unequivocally called unwholesome, or such like. This is perhaps not unlike the English term "love", one translation of "pema" I have heard. In English, it seems love can refer both to an instinctive and involuntary emotion, often found between family and friends; this love might often be primarily motivated by the pleasure offer of the object, and might appear partial. Love might also refer to an acquired and proactive emotion, as in religious contexts, or in some social movements, motivated more by something intrinsic to the object, less its benefit to the subject, such as innate dignity or moral worth. 
In some passages, it seems "pema" (especially in regard to a teacher) is spoken of positively. In others, it seemes to be placed on a par with "chandas" and "rāga" (SN 22.87).
