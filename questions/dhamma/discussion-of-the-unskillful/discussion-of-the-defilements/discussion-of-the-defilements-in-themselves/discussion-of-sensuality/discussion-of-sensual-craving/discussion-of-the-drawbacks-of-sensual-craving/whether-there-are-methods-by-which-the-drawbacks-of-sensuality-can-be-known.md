# Whether there are methods by which the drawbacks of sensuality can be known?

## Objections

## Replies

Quite useful are:

1. Similes, especially that of the leper, or the dog and the rack of bones, or that of someone drinking salt water, or of potato chips (Tan Ajaan Geoff). 

2. Reflection on the times when a strong craving was frustrated, which is a universal experience.

3. Reflection on those who have abandoned large amounts of craving.
