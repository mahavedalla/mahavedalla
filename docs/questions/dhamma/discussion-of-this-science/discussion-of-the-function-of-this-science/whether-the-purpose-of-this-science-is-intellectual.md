# Whether the purpose of this science is intellectual?

## Bibliography

<!-- 

Notes:

At this stage, this question does not yet have a clear answer to me, as "this science" is an ongoing development at present, and is thus somewhat undetermined around the edges. There is a sense in which the proximate purpose of this science is intellectual: to wit, the systematic and perspicuous exposition of Dhamma and Vinaya. Nonetheless, as I intend it now, this purpose is subordinate and instrumental to the final end of Nibbāna, which is not currently conceived as intellectual.
