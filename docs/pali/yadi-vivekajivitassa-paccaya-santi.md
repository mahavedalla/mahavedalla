# Atthi te dhamme, yehi asamannāgato nālaṃ kāyavivekasmiṃ viharituṃ?

Pañha: "Atthi te dhamme, yehi puggalo asamannāgato nālaṃ kāyavivekasmiṃ viharitun?"ti
Paṭiññā: “Atthī”ti.

## Atthitakaṇḍa

Tāva, yaṃ atthi evarūpā dhamme, taṃ patiṭṭhahāmi, pacchā te dhamme vibhajissāmi.

Yaṃ atthi evarūpā dhamme, taṃ catubbidhaṃ veditabbaṃ: araññasuttaṃ, meghiyasuttaṃ, upālisuttaṃ, samaññaphalasuttaṃ. Apica, yasmā paṭiññā yamhā araññasuttamattamhāpi daṭṭhabbā, tasmā taṃyeva ete kaṇḍe kathetabbaṃ. 

Idaṃ araññasutte "kāmavitakkena, byāpādavitakkena, vihiṃsāvitakkena, duppañño hoti jaḷo elamūgo – imehi kho, bhikkhave, catūhi dhammehi samannāgato bhikkhu nālaṃ araññavanappatthāni pantāni senāsanāni paṭisevitun"tiādinā evaṃ vuttanayena veditabbo.

Kasmā atthi te dhamme yehi puggalo asamannāgato nālaṃ kāyavivekasmiṃ viharituṃ? Yasmā, yo puggalo evarūpehi dhammehi asammanāgato kāyavivekasmiṃ viharati, so cittakhepo bhaveyya vā saṃsīdeyya vā uplaveyya vā, yathāha aṅguttaranikāye dasakanipāte upālisutte: "ekatte haranti maññe mano vanāni samādhiṃ alabhamānassa bhikkhuno. yo kho, upāli, evaṃ vadeyya – ‘ahaṃ samādhiṃ alabhamāno araññavanapatthāni pantāni senāsanāni paṭisevissāmī’ti, tassetaṃ pāṭikaṅkhaṃ – ‘saṃsīdissati vā uplavissati vā’tī"tiādi (AN 10.99). 

## Vibhaṅgakaṇḍa

1. Nekkhammavitakka
2. Abyāpādavitakka
3. Avihiṃsāvitakka
4. Paññā
5. The noble aggregate of virtue
6. Noble sense-restraint
7. Noble mindfulness and clear comprehension
8. Samādhi
9. Non-delight in company
