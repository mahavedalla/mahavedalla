# Whether abstention from all sensual pleasures is functionally necessary for the abandonment of all craving?

## Bibliography

Notes:

One line of argument: the dhutangas are not practically necessary; abstention from all sensual pleasures is stronger than even the dhutangas; thus (relevant premises assumed), neither is such abstention practically necessary.

Moreover, the wealth of evidence suggesting some enjoyment of sensual (or sensorial) pleasures is permitted or even encouraged (nature), even for monks. The abstention which is the subject of this question could be called "unrestricted abstention".
