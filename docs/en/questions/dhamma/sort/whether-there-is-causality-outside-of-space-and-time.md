# Whether there is causality outside of space and time?

## Bibliography

<!-- 

Notes:

Anecdotally, in a Q&A between 1-5-26 and 2-10-26, Tan Ajaan explicitly denied that there was causality outside of space and time. Would this not present further problems for his view of Nibbāna, unlike Albahari's where Nibbāna is not only causal, but fundamental to ordinary experience? If awakened awareness is outside of space and time, and awakened awareness knows that Nibbāna, Arahantship, the goal, etc.,  has been attained, and that knowledge is not caused by anything outside of space and time, this seems to exclude even Albahari's view of extrinsic cognition.
