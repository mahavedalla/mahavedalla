# Whether the mind absent craving is innately happy and peaceful?

## Bibliography

<!-- 

Notes:

AN 1.49: "pabhassaramidaṃ, bhikkhave, cittaṃ. tañca kho āgantukehi upakkilesehi upakkiliṭṭhaṃ."

This question should be disentangled from similar questions descending from different traditions about Buddha-nature, and so on. The question is plainly whether the mind independent of craving is happy, peaceful, content, and so on. Perhaps the motivation for the question is related to others which address whether there is any such qualities in the absence of craving. It might be though the mind here is analogous to the body in that the body freed from afflictions is healthy, and so the mind freed from defilements is happy. Whether this is a good analogy is uncertain to me.
